Cleanup safety steps from public health agencies

It is general information from federal public health pages, not medical advice, and your own doctor always comes first.

  • Disturbing dry droppings is what causes trouble, so keep brooms and shop vacuums out of the picture.
  • Ventilation is step one for a musty shed or crawl space: about thirty minutes of open doors.
  • Germs are invisible, so gloves on, then soap and water when you finish.
  • Cover the waste and the surface beneath it in disinfectant, give it about five minutes, then wipe up using disposable towels.
  • Place used towels and any carcass in a plastic bag, close it tightly, and keep skin from touching the animal.
  • Fever, aches or breathing difficulty after contact with waste deserve a physician's attention. We make no claims about anyone's health.
