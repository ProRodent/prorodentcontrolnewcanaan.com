Cleanup safety steps from public health agencies

The points below restate public CDC advice in plain words, and they hold even if you never hire anyone.

  • Leave dry pellets and nest bits alone until they are wet, and do not blow, brush or vacuum them.
  • Ventilation is step one for a musty shed or crawl space: about thirty minutes of open doors.
  • Disposable gloves and reusable rubber ones both work. Wash the gloves, then yourself.
  • Nobody should touch droppings until they are completely wet and have rested about five minutes.
  • Pull the bag tight and knot it before it goes out, and keep your hands covered whenever a dead rodent has to move.
  • Fever, body aches or breathing trouble in the weeks afterward belong with a physician, who should also hear that rodent waste was nearby. Our trade is pests, not medicine.
