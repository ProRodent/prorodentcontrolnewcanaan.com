Handling rodent waste with no dust in the air: what to do

The points below restate public CDC advice in plain words, and they hold even if you never hire anyone.

  • Disturbing dry droppings is what causes trouble, so keep brooms and shop vacuums out of the picture.
  • If a space has been shut for weeks, throw it open and walk away for roughly thirty minutes.
  • Disposable gloves and reusable rubber ones both work. Wash the gloves, then yourself.
  • Cover the waste and the surface beneath it in disinfectant, give it about five minutes, then wipe up using disposable towels.
  • Choose a sealed bag over an open bin for waste, and use gloves or a tool on a carcass.
  • Not well a few weeks later? A clinician is the one to ask, so describe the rodent contact to them.
