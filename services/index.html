How one service leads to the next.

Jobs usually run in five steps: inspection, trapping, sealing, cleanup and repair. Cleanup waits for sealing, since an attic scrubbed while animals can still enter will only be fouled again.

Many people need one step only. An owner who has already closed every gap might want just cleanup, and a shopkeeper might want only monitoring. Tell us what is done, and the visit covers what is left.

We run no special emergency tier, since urgency leaves the work itself unchanged. Phones are picked up all night, and any page here can lead to a same-day request if the day has room.

Service questions, answered.

Which page do I start with?

Start with inspection if you are not sure what you have. That visit names the animal, finds the entries and produces a plan before you commit to anything larger.

Is it possible to book only sealing, or only cleanup?

Yes. Tell us what has already been done, and the visit is built around what remains.

Are vehicles and yards included?

They are. One page deals with engine bays and cabin vents, and another handles burrows, sheds, shrubs and feeders.

Is there something for businesses?

Shops, offices and restaurants will want the commercial page, which covers regular checks, treatment, and written notes left after every stop.
