While you wait: what to do and what to avoid

Pitched at householders, the list below keeps contaminated dust out of the air while you wait for an appointment.

  • Leave dry pellets and nest bits alone until they are wet, and do not blow, brush or vacuum them.
  • Ventilation is step one for a musty shed or crawl space: about thirty minutes of open doors.
  • Surfaces near waste can carry germs you cannot see, which is why gloves and handwashing both matter.
  • Wet the droppings and the surface beneath with a household disinfectant, or bleach cut to its label strength, then give it about five minutes before wiping up with paper towels.
  • Pull the bag tight and knot it before it goes out, and keep your hands covered whenever a dead rodent has to move.
  • Watch your own health for several weeks. If a fever or breathlessness appears, give your doctor the full story of what you handled.
